Welcome to Tanikella

This website is a growing record of Tanikella’s heritage, places of worship, local traditions, and shared memories.

We invite residents, elders, and people from Tanikella living elsewhere to contribute photographs, verified facts, and personal recollections. Together, we can create a useful record for the next generation.
