Village overview

About Tanikella (Thanikella)

Tanikella, also written as Thanikella, is a prominent village in Konijerla Mandal of Khammam district, Telangana, India.

Location and access

Location & connectivity

The village is approximately 13–14 km east of Khammam city. It is connected via NH365A, NH365BG, and State Highway SH-42.

Geography and postal details

Geography & postal code

Elevation: 107 metres above sea level (as provided). Postal PIN code: 507305.
